Solmar City is an original coastal open-world action game. Explore its streets by foot or car, raise the heat, and take on six linked jobs with Jax and fixer Ray. The city and its cast are procedurally drawn and the game runs offline from static files.

## Run

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080.

Controls: WASD walk or drive, mouse look, Shift run, Space jump, E enter or exit a car, click shoot, F punch, P pause.
